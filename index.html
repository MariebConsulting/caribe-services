GOOD QUESTIONS

Wondering about the details?

Here's what most homeowners want to know before the first shovel hits the ground.

Does an in-ground trampoline need drainage?+

Drainage is a critical site consideration. Depending on soil, grading, and groundwater, a project may call for a free-draining base, piping, a sump system, or other measures. We review those conditions before determining an approach.

Can you install a trampoline I've already purchased?+

Tell us the make and model when you reach out. We can review the manufacturer's installation requirements and determine whether the site and scope are a fit.

Will the trampoline sit flush with my lawn?+

The goal is a clean, ground-level appearance. Final frame height, support conditions, edge details, and clearances follow the selected manufacturer's requirements and the site's drainage plan.

Can you help with a fire pit or walkway too?+

Yes. Tell us which additional projects you're considering. Coordinating excavation and access can make it easier to plan several backyard improvements together.

Are in-ground trampolines risk-free?+

No trampoline is risk-free. A ground-level configuration reduces the height of the entry edge, but safe use still requires appropriate clearances, supervision, maintenance, and following the manufacturer's safety instructions.
